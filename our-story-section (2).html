Our story

How a family farm became a beekeeping operation worth defending.

Keep scrolling →

2019

We bought a small farm in Keller.

As the land around us disappeared into subdivisions and retail developments, we made a deliberate choice: keep this ground in agriculture.

Then

Honey turned into something bigger.

Low land disturbance. High environmental leverage. And a natural fit with education, stewardship and community.

Next

Landowners started asking for help.

Placing hives gave us more diverse forage and helped families protect their land from development pressure.

Now

Four kids who've grown up in the hives.

They've been stung more times than we can count. This isn't a side project we'll sell off someday. It's the operation we're building together.
